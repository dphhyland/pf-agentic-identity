# The lifecycle listener: a start-up audit banner in every war, and a clean shutdown of what each war opened

## Changelog

- Plan item F-2: `libs/platform-pf` gains `LifecycleListener`, registered by name in `pf-runtime.war` (through
  `build/pingfederate/filters.xml`, which the war assembler writes into its `web.xml`), in `oidf.war` and in
  `gm-api.war`. At start-up it marks the war's copy of platform as the webapp's, registers its metrics MXBean and logs
  one start-up audit banner at INFO after the war's filters and load-on-startup servlets have started; at undeploy it
  closes that copy's managed executors, MXBean and Redis pools within five seconds. It touches only a copy of platform
  its own war's classloader loaded.
- `gm-api.war` bundles `platform-pf` and `platform` in its `WEB-INF/lib`, so it has its own components, metrics and
  banner, and its own health at `/gm-api/agentic-identity/health/{live,ready}`; its two load-on-startup servlets
  register the component `GM_API`.
- New in the register: F-0210, F-0211 and U-0220. Closed: U-0024 (a `@WebListener` in a `WEB-INF/lib` jar runs in
  `pf-runtime.war` without a `web.xml` entry; one in `server/default/deploy` runs nowhere).

## Notes

Operators see a banner in server.log each time a war starts - `Start-up audit for / (ROOT):` for `pf-runtime.war`,
`Start-up audit for /gm-api (Grant Management API):` for gm-api - followed by one line each for this repository's
version, the commit (`unknown` until a build records it, F-0190), PingFederate's and Java's versions, the deployment
profile and how `OIDF_DEPLOYMENT_PROFILE` set it, the topology (`standalone` until 0.7.0), the accepted risks, how many
`OIDF_ACCEPTED_RISKS` entries were not accepted (each also logged at WARN, naming it), every setting that turned TLS
certificate checking off, whether the JVM's HTTP client checks host names, each component's state and reason, the
background jobs, the metrics MXBean's name and where the war's copy of platform was loaded from.
[docs/operator/startup-audit.md](../../operator/startup-audit.md) explains each line. Nothing is refused or changed
because of what the banner shows, and there is nothing to configure: refusing components for it is PR-5 (0.6.0).

A consumer that composes its own image context and copies `filters.xml` gets the listener with it; the assembler
refuses the war if no staged jar holds the listener's class, which `platform-pf`'s jar does (staged since F-1). A
consumer that deploys `gm-api.war` gets two more jars inside it and nothing else to do.

Give PingFederate ten seconds or more to stop. The rig's container was created with a one-second stop timeout, so a
plain `docker stop` killed PingFederate (exit 137); `docker stop -t 30` stopped it cleanly in about five seconds
(F-0211). The listener's shutdown line appears when a war is undeployed, not when PingFederate stops, because
PingFederate stops its logging first (F-0210).

Verified on 2026-09-28. Unit tests on JDK 17.0.11 and 20: two classloader copies of platform and platform-pf, where
the listener marks, audits once and shuts down only the copy its war loaded and a listener handed another loader's
copy leaves it alone; the banner's lines and the one-line rule; the assembler's tests against the shipped
`filters.xml` with the listener; gm-api's `GM_API` part. On the rig (PingFederate 13.1.3.0, conformance profile, the
image's java 21.0.12.1): one banner per boot for `/ (ROOT)`, in eight boots, logged after every filter and
load-on-startup servlet had registered its component and just before Jetty reported the context started, with
`platform` loaded from the war's own `WEB-INF/lib`; the engine's copy in `server/default/deploy` logged nothing;
`gm-api.war` dropped into `server/default/deploy` logged its own banner with `GM_API READY` and answered its live and
ready with 200, and removing it logged `Lifecycle listener: /gm-api (Grant Management API) stopped; closed 1` with
its MXBean unregistered.
