# Metrics in platform: bounded counters, timers and gauges, one MXBean per loaded copy

## Changelog

- Plan item O-3: `libs/platform` gains `platform.metrics` - `Metrics.counter`, `timer` and `gauge`, each
  registered once with fixed, bounded labels (a declared set, or a cap past which values count as `other` and a
  fold counter rises), timers with a count, sum, max and fifteen fixed buckets from 1 ms to 60 s, and a lock-free
  hot path. Each loaded copy of platform registers one MXBean, `com.pingidentity.ps.oidf:type=Metrics,copy=...`,
  unregistered through its lifecycle. Nothing registers a metric yet: O-4 and C-3 are the first users, O-5 the
  Prometheus endpoint. New in the register: F-0170, U-0180.

## Before you deploy

None.

## Notes

Nothing to configure, and nothing changes on the wire or in the logs: no code on main registers a metric, so no
MXBean appears until O-4 (the event counter) or C-3 (the executors' per-run metrics) lands. Once one does, a JMX
view exists - one MXBean for each copy of platform that has a metric, the webapp's, the engine's, a plugin's
relocated copy and a separate war's, each under its own `copy` key - with the counts, every metric as open data
and a flat table of samples named as Prometheus lines. The webapp's MXBean is unregistered when F-2's listener
shuts its lifecycle down at undeploy; the engine's, where the OGNL criteria run, stays until the JVM stops, so
their decisions stay visible. The [platform README](../../../libs/platform/README.md#metrics) has the bounds, the
buckets and why, what O-5 renders, the cost, and how O-4 counts events.

Bounds: 256 metrics and 16384 series a registry, 2048 series a metric, 1024 values a label, 128 characters a
value. MetricRegistryTest passes 100,000 distinct label values through a capped and a declared label, and
100,000 distinct names through a registry, and each stops at its bound. ClassLoaderCopiesTest loads platform twice
in one JVM through two URLClassLoaders and shows two MXBean names, two registries, and each lifecycle removing
only its own; MXBeanRegistrationTest reads the attributes as open types and rebuilds the snapshots through a
proxy. The new decision methods are in platform's 100% METHOD gate. Verified on 2026-09-28 with `mvn verify` on
the module under JDK 17 and JDK 20, and the metrics tests under the pinned PingFederate image's own java
21.0.12.1. Not seen yet: an MXBean in a running PingFederate (U-0180). O-5's endpoint can reach the engine's
metrics only through the MBean server, which the classloader rules do not yet list (F-0170). F-0027, the
observability umbrella, stays open.
