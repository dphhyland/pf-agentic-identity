# Attestation on every endpoint that authenticates a client, and details held to the ceiling where they arrive

## Changelog

- `ClientAttestationAuth` is mapped over PingFederate's CIBA backchannel endpoint (`/as/bc-auth.ciba`), device
  authorization endpoint (`/as/device_authz.oauth2`), introspection and revocation as well as the token and PAR
  endpoints, so a client whose only credential is its attestation can authenticate wherever PingFederate authenticates
  a client (plan item S4d). At introspection and revocation it authenticates and bridges only. The war assembler
  refuses a war unless `Fapi2Profile` runs before it on every path they share, automatic registration before it at the
  token endpoint and front-channel registration before it at PAR and the authorization endpoint.
- At the token endpoint, PAR, CIBA and the device endpoint the filter grants `authorize(requested, attestation,
  INHERIT)` and forwards **the granted details**, with the verified agent, in place of what the client sent: a field
  the attestation constrains and the request leaves out takes the attestation's value. A request that asks for no
  details forwards none. PingFederate issues what PAR and CIBA stored and ignores the token request's parameter on
  those grants, so this is what holds the code and CIBA grants to the ceiling (F-0032, with S4D3's belt and criterion
  to come). The token gate in libs/client-attestation has an `INHERIT` entry point for it
  (`ClientAttestationVerifier.verify(..., Omission)`); the OGNL criterion stays strict.
- A signed request object at PAR or CIBA must carry details within the attestation's as they stand (400
  `invalid_authorization_details`); one the filter cannot read is 400 `invalid_request_object`, and a `request_uri` at
  PAR is 400 `invalid_request` (RFC 9126 §2.1). A repeated `authorization_details`, `oidf_requested_access`, `request`
  or `request_uri` is 400 `invalid_request`.
- At the authorization endpoint, `authorization_details` (or a request object carrying them) from a client with
  `attestation_required=true` that did not come through PAR is refused with the error page, never a redirect.
- `attestation_required=true` is enforced at every endpoint the filter authenticates at, not only the token endpoint.

## Before you deploy

1. **Attestation now authenticates at CIBA, the device endpoint, introspection and revocation too.** From 0.6.0 the
   filter runs at `/as/bc-auth.ciba`, `/as/device_authz.oauth2`, `/as/introspect.oauth2` and
   `/as/revoke_token.oauth2`. A client that authenticated there with its own `private_key_jwt` (or secret) and also
   sends `OAuth-Client-Attestation` headers is now verified and bridged: its attestation must verify, its attester must
   be bound to it and it must have a bridge key in `OIDF_BRIDGE_SIGNING_KEYS`, or the request is refused 401
   `invalid_client` where it used to pass on the client's own credential. One that sends no attestation headers is
   untouched, unless it has `attestation_required=true`, which is now refused 401 `invalid_client` at these endpoints
   as at the token endpoint. Why: RFC 9126 §2.1 and RFC 8628 §3.1 have a client authenticate at these endpoints as at
   the token endpoint, and a client whose only credential is its attestation could not. How to tell: the filter's log
   line "attest_jwt_client_auth: rejected" and `attestation.client.refused` events naming these endpoints' clients.
   What to change: give every client that sends the headers at these endpoints a bridge key and an attester binding,
   or stop it sending the headers there; give a resource server that introspects with `attestation_required=true` an
   attestation, or clear the property. There is no development-profile escape: the filter either authenticates a
   request that carries an attestation or refuses it, and passing one through unverified is what the filter exists to
   stop.
2. **Details at PAR and CIBA are held to the attestation ceiling where they arrive.** From 0.6.0 an attested request's
   `authorization_details` at PAR, CIBA, the device endpoint and the token endpoint are granted against the
   attestation's and PingFederate receives the granted details, not the client's. A client that asks for more than its
   attestation allows gets what it gets today at the token endpoint, now at PAR and CIBA as well: 400
   `invalid_authorization_details` for a detail outside the attestation's, before PingFederate stores anything. A
   client that leaves a field out gets the attestation's value for it, where the token endpoint used to refuse the
   omission: a `sales_agent` detail without `max_txn_eur` under an attestation allowing 500 is granted 500, and the
   details PingFederate stores and issues are the granted ones, in canonical JSON (members
   by name, plain decimals). A signed request object cannot be rewritten, so its details must restate every field the
   attestation constrains and stay within it. Why: PingFederate issues what PAR and CIBA stored whatever the token
   request says (seen on the rig, 2026-09-30), so a client could push details outside its attestation and leave the
   parameter out at the token endpoint (F-0032). How to tell: 400 `invalid_authorization_details` at PAR or CIBA where
   the token endpoint used to refuse, and tokens whose `authorization_details` carry fields the request did not send.
   What to change: nothing for a client within its attestation; a client relying on details outside it must have its
   attestation widened by its attester. A resource server that compared the issued details with what the client asked
   for byte for byte must compare the parsed JSON. There is no development-profile escape: containment is the
   control, and production has no report mode.
3. **An attestation-required client must use PAR to send `authorization_details`.** From 0.6.0 an authorization
   request that names a client with `attestation_required=true` and carries `authorization_details`, a request object
   with them (or one the filter cannot read) or a `request_uri` that is not a PAR reference is refused 400
   `invalid_request` with the error page (`OIDF_FEDERATION_ERROR_PAGE`, or the built-in one), never a redirect. Why: the
   front channel carries no attestation, so details sent there are held to nothing and PingFederate issues them later.
   How to tell: the page "this client must send authorization_details in a pushed authorization request (PAR)" and an
   `attestation.client.refused` event with `invalid_request`. What to change: push these clients' authorization
   requests (PingFederate's "Require Pushed Authorization Requests" on the client or the server does it for every
   request). There is no development-profile escape, for the reason "Details at PAR and CIBA are held to the
   attestation ceiling where they arrive" gives.

## Notes

Verified first on the rig (slot 1, pfai-p3-s4d1, PingFederate 13.1.3, origin/main 6606b141, 2026-09-30), with the
bridge assertion and no new filter: PAR, `/as/bc-auth.ciba`, the device endpoint, introspection and revocation accept
the bridge assertion (U-0020, closed); PingFederate ignores the token-time parameter on the code and CIBA grants in
both directions (U-0019, left open for S4D3); `/as/device_authz.oauth2` authenticates clients and takes
`authorization_details`, so the filter is mapped there too, which the plan did not list; PingFederate serves CIBA at
`/as/bc-auth.ciba;x`, `%2E` and dot-segment spellings and not with a trailing slash or another case (U-0094's notes).

Then with the filter (commit f687b25c), on the same rig: an attested PAR asking for `sales_agent` [EMEA] under a
ceiling of [EMEA, AMER] up to 500, redeemed with no parameter, was issued [EMEA] up to 500; [APAC] was refused 400 at
PAR; an attested PAR with no details issued none; attested CIBA with `[{"type":"sales_agent"}]` was issued the whole
ceiling and with no details none; `/as/bc-auth.ciba;x` over the ceiling was refused by the filter; introspection and
revocation served attestation-authenticated clients; the device endpoint refused details over the ceiling; a signed
request object within the ceiling was issued as signed, one omitting a constrained field or outside the ceiling was
refused; and with `attestation_required=true`, `authorization_details` at the authorization endpoint without PAR got
the 400 page and no redirect, while the same client's attested PAR and its `request_uri` were served. The rig is
down. The device grant's token was not driven (the verification page's confirmation step was not scripted, U-0330).

Tests: ClientAttestationIssuedDetailsTest (the rewrite at each endpoint over a table of requested, ceiling and
granted details, the omitted parameter, request objects, the no-PAR refusal), the INHERIT cases of the shared vector
file through the token gate (AsVectorRunnerTest), and the assembler's chains and order rules (AssemblerTest,
DeclarationTest, and StockWarGoldenTest against 13.1.3's stock war). pf-integration's 806 tests pass on JDK 17 with its
coverage gate and on JDK 21 (temurin 21.0.12).

Residual: F-0032 stays open until S4D3 adds the response belt and the issuance criterion. Fapi2Profile is not mapped
over the device endpoint (F-0320). A client's `attestation_required` is read through the 30 s policy cache, so a
federation RP registered at the authorization endpoint by an earlier request in the last 30 s may be read as not
required until the cache turns over (F-0300).
