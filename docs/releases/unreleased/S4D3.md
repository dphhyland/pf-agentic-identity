# The details a token carries are held to the attestation ceiling, and a bare refresh of a payment grant is decided again

## Changelog

- A new OGNL issuance criterion, `@com.pingidentity.ps.oidf.servlet.clientregistration.utils.IssuedDetailsCriterion@withinCeiling(#this)`,
  holds what an access-token mapping is about to issue - PingFederate's `context.OAuthAuthorizationDetails` - to the
  client attestation's `authorization_details` by the model's strict `contains`, on the client-credentials, code,
  CIBA, device and refresh grants the rig drove, and on any other mapping it is added to (plan item S4d, F-0032, F-0033). A request with no attestation passes
  unless its client has `attestation_required=true`; the criterion never throws, and answers `false` while
  `ATTESTATION_AUTH` is failed or refused.
- The same criterion refuses a refresh that sends no `authorization_details` for a grant holding a detail of a type in
  the new setting `OIDF_ATTESTATION_REDECIDE_ON_REFRESH_TYPES` (default `payment_initiation,account_information`), so
  the client repeats its details and PingFederate asks the RAR processor, and the PDP, again (F-0105).
- A response belt, `IssuedDetailsBelt`, is mapped over `/as/token.oauth2` just before ClientAttestationAuth: for an
  attested request it holds the token response (up to 64 KiB) until the `authorization_details` in it are within the
  ceiling, and replaces it with 400 `invalid_authorization_details` when they are not, revoking the grant behind the
  response's refresh token. A larger success is 500 `server_error`. Other requests pass untouched and unbuffered.
- Each refusal is `attestation.issued.refused` in pf-integration's `attestation` catalogue, with `enforcer` and
  `detail_types` (never a value).
- The war assembler checks one more order rule, IssuedDetailsBelt before ClientAttestationAuth.
- [docs/operator/issued-details.md](../../operator/issued-details.md) describes both checks, the refresh rule and the
  residual risks.

## Before you deploy

1. **Add the issued-details criterion to your access-token mappings.** From 0.6.0, add
   `@com.pingidentity.ps.oidf.servlet.clientregistration.utils.IssuedDetailsCriterion@withinCeiling(#this)` as an
   expression criterion, with an error result of your choosing, to every access-token mapping an attested client can
   get a token through - usually the `Default` mapping (code, CIBA, device and refresh grants), the `Client Credentials`
   mapping and any token-exchange mapping. Write the `@class@method` form: `IssuedDetailsCriterion.withinCeiling(#this)`
   fails in PingFederate 13.1.3 and refuses every token. Why: the code, CIBA and device grants issue the details stored
   earlier, and a refresh issues the grant's, so the ceiling checked against the token request's parameter alone could be
   stepped round by leaving the parameter out (F-0032, the review's most serious finding); CAS §7.1 says the
   authorization server "MUST ... ensure that any authority granted in issued tokens is a subset of the attestation's
   authorization_details". What it refuses: a token for an attested client carrying a detail not within the
   attestation's (a field the ceiling constrains missing included), a token for a client with `attestation_required`
   presented without an attestation at the token endpoint, the bare refresh in "A refresh of a payment or account grant
   must repeat its `authorization_details`", and every token
   while `ATTESTATION_AUTH` is failed. PingFederate answers the refusal with 400 `invalid_grant` and your error result as
   its description. How to tell: after adding it, look in server.log for `attestation.issued.refused` with
   `enforcer=issuance_criterion`, and for "Authorization failed. The following criterion was not met" from
   `TokenAuthorizationIssuanceCriteriaChecker`. A client with no attestation keeps getting what it was issued before.
   Development-profile escape: none - containment has no report mode.

2. **Tokens over the attestation ceiling are now refused at the token endpoint.** From 0.6.0 the token endpoint's
   response belt reads the `authorization_details` in each token response to an attested client and, when they are not
   within the attestation's, the client gets 400 `invalid_authorization_details` with "the issued authorization_details
   exceed what the client attestation allows" instead of its tokens; a success body over 64 KiB, which cannot be
   checked, is 500 `server_error`. Why: the belt holds what is issued even on a mapping without the criterion. PingFederate
   has made the tokens by then: the grant behind the refresh token is revoked, and a JWT access token, which PingFederate
   cannot revoke, stays valid until it expires but never reaches the client. How to tell: `attestation.issued.refused`
   with `enforcer=response_belt` in server.log, and 400 `invalid_authorization_details` at `/as/token.oauth2`. What to
   change: nothing in PingFederate; a client refused here was being issued more than its attester vouched for, so the
   attester's `authorization_details`, or what the client pushed at PAR or CIBA before 0.6.0, is what to look at. A
   client whose attestation carries no `authorization_details` is refused any details, as at the token gate.
   Development-profile escape: none - containment has no report mode.

3. **A refresh of a payment or account grant must repeat its `authorization_details`.** From 0.6.0 the criterion in "Add
   the issued-details criterion to your access-token mappings" refuses a refresh token request that sends no `authorization_details` for a grant holding a
   `payment_initiation` or `account_information` detail - the types in `OIDF_ATTESTATION_REDECIDE_ON_REFRESH_TYPES`,
   comma-separated - for every client, attested or not. Why: PingFederate reissues a bare refresh's stored details
   without asking the RAR processor, so the PDP never sees a policy change until the grant ends (F-0105); a refresh that
   repeats its details is decided again. RFC 9396 §7 leaves the bare refresh to the authorization server: "If the
   client does not specify the authorization_details token request parameters, the AS determines the resulting
   authorization_details at its discretion." How to tell: before upgrading, find clients that refresh payment or
   account grants without `authorization_details`; after, `attestation.issued.refused` with reason
   `redecide_on_refresh`, and 400 `invalid_grant` for their bare refreshes. The refresh token is not revoked. What to
   change: the client sends the grant's `authorization_details` with each refresh (the rig's CIBA payment grant was
   served that way and the PDP asked again); or narrow the setting's types, or set it blank to send no refresh back,
   which leaves F-0105's risk as it was. Only mappings that carry the criterion apply it. Development-profile escape:
   none - it is the setting.

## Notes

Verified first on the rig (slot 2, pfai-p3-s4d3, PingFederate 13.1.3, origin/main cf5b8bce, 2026-09-30), with OGNL
expression attributes on both access-token mappings read back from the issued JWT: `context.OAuthAuthorizationDetails`
is an `AttributeValue` whose object values are one `LinkedHashMap` per detail about to be issued, numbers as PingFederate's
JSON reader made them (a `Double` for a decimal), on the client-credentials, code, CIBA, device and refresh grants
(U-0018, closed); the criterion sees the grant type and whether the request carried `authorization_details` through
`context.HttpRequest`, so the refresh rule was built (F-0105 closed); PingFederate compresses no token response
whatever `Accept-Encoding` says (U-0029, closed); the device grant, driven headless for the first time, issued what the
device endpoint stored, as S4D1 found for the code and CIBA grants (U-0019, closed).

Then with the belt and the criterion (the rig's results are in docs/operator/issued-details.md, On the rig): a code
grant pushed under one ceiling and redeemed under a narrower one was 400 from the belt with the criterion off and
`invalid_grant` from the criterion with it on; CIBA the same from the belt; the grants within the ceiling were issued; a
bare refresh of a CIBA payment grant was refused and a refresh repeating the details was issued with the stub PDP asked
again. PingFederate also asks access-token mapping criteria when the authorization endpoint resumes, so the criterion
decides an attestation-required client at the token endpoint only. The rig is down and its keys deleted.

Tests: IssuedDetailsCriterionTest (each grant over a table of issued details, the markers, a verification for another
client, no attestation with and without `attestation_required`, the authorization endpoint, the refresh rule per listed
type with and without the parameter, failed and disabled components, the criterion's own verification, the models not
loading, and a copy on another classloader), IssuedDetailsBeltTest (within, over, over 64 KiB, exactly 64 KiB, encoded,
unverified, not attested, errors, a large error, no details, unreadable details, the revocation failing) and the
assembler's chain, order rule and golden descriptor (AssemblerTest, DeclarationTest, StockWarGoldenTest against 13.1.3's
stock war). pf-integration's tests (877 at a2fadea7) and the assembler's 82 pass on JDK 17 with their coverage gates, and on
JDK 21 (temurin 21.0.12); the belt's test for a response committed past it came after, and passes on JDK 17 and in CI.

Residual: a refused JWT access token stays valid until it expires, never delivered (PingFederate has no seam to revoke
one); the belt cannot see values RFC 9396 §7 lets an authorization server omit from the response, which PingFederate
13.1.3 did not omit. The criterion is the control; the belt is its fallback.
