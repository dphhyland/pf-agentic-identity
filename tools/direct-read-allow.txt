# The direct reads tools/direct-read-scan.py admits outside platform (plan item ST-6): each, and why.
#
# Two forms, each under a `# group <name>` line (the scan's docstring has the rules):
#   <module>: <why it is never shipped>               in the "not shipped" group only; admits every hit in the module
#   <path> | <pattern> | <finding id, or why>         a hit in that file whose line contains the pattern
# CI runs the scan with --check-allow-list, so a line that admits nothing fails the build: when you convert a read,
# delete its line; when a finding here closes, its lines go with it (the scan refuses a closed finding's id).
# A new direct read is a failure until it is converted - read it through platform.settings - or admitted here with
# a finding or a reason a reviewer accepts.
